Adaptation for Scuderia Ferrari Club Paris prototype (2026-10-21):
Merged coincident vertices to identify original physical panels; grouped existing door skins, mirrors, handles and inner trim into conventional hinged door assemblies; isolated front bonnet and rear deck/engine cover; created four named hinge pivots; retained original mesh surfaces/materials and the four wheel groups; re-exported as Draco-compressed glTF 2.0.
No new Ferrari model shape was authored.
