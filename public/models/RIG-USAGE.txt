FERRARI F40 — SCROLL RIG

Source and license: see ATTRIBUTION.txt.
Rebuild with Blender 4.1+: blender -b --python scripts/prepare-f40-rig.py -- <source.glb> <output.glb>
The source GLB is available at https://sceneview.github.io/models/platforms/ferrari_f40.glb

The exported glTF is Y-up; nose along negative Z; closed hinges use identity rotation.
Opening angles in radians:
- hinge_left: rotation.y = -1.08
- hinge_right: rotation.y = +1.08
- hinge_front: rotation.x = -1.0
- hinge_rear: rotation.x = +1.1

Original 245,552 triangles, authored split normals and UVs retained. Physical panel
partitions derived from source indexed topology, without welding or decimation.
Cockpit, V8 and suspension are part of the original model.
Fallback poster is a native Blender render of this same F40.
